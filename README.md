# Free Software Alternatives

> An open, source-linked list of free and open-source replacements for paid design, photo, video and CAD software. Maintained by **[FreeInsteadOf.com](https://freeinsteadof.com)**.

**Full comparisons, test notes and migration tips: [freeinsteadof.com](https://freeinsteadof.com)**

Each entry links to the vendor's site, the page the price was read from, the platforms, and whether the tool was **tested** (installed and used to export a real file on Linux) or reviewed from its official site only. A few entries are paid, one-time-purchase options. They are listed for comparison and the price column marks them as paid.

## Free alternatives to After Effects

*Category: Video · Prices checked 2026-10-14 · [FreeInsteadOf.com →](https://freeinsteadof.com/)*

Natron and Blender cover free compositing and motion graphics we rendered; Resolve Fusion, Cavalry and Jitter are strong docs-backed options depending on VFX vs motion-design focus.

| Tool | Price | Platforms | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Natron](https://natrongithub.github.io/) ✅ 🔓 | Free (open source) ([source](https://github.com/NatronGitHub/Natron/releases)) | Linux, Windows, macOS | Node-based 2D compositing, keying, roto and tracking |
| [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) ✅ 🔓 | Free (open source, GPL) ([source](https://www.blender.org/about/license/)) | Windows, macOS, Linux | 3D motion graphics, animated titles and VFX with a built-in video editor |
| [DaVinci Resolve (Fusion page)](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve/fusion) | Free; DaVinci Resolve Studio $295 ([source](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/products/davinciresolve)) | Windows, macOS, Linux | Editors who want node-based VFX and motion graphics inside their editing app |
| [Cavalry](https://cavalry.scenegroup.co/) | Free for individuals ([source](https://cavalry.scenegroup.co/pricing)) | Windows, macOS | Procedural 2D motion design and Lottie export |
| [Jitter](https://jitter.video/) | Free plan (720p/30fps export); paid Pro, Max, Ultra, Enterprise ([source](https://jitter.video/pricing/)) | Web browser | Quick UI and social animations in the browser, starting from Figma designs |

## Free alternatives to Figma

*Category: Design · Prices checked 2026-10-14 · [FreeInsteadOf.com →](https://freeinsteadof.com/)*

For a free Figma alternative you can use offline on Linux today, Inkscape covers vector UI frames we exported; Penpot is the open-source collaborative closest match (docs-only here); Lunacy and Affinity add desktop options with free tiers per their sites.

| Tool | Price | Platforms | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Penpot](https://penpot.app/) 🔓 | Free Professional ($0/user/month); Unlimited $7/editor/month (cap $175/month) ([source](https://penpot.app/pricing)) | Web browser, Self-hosted | Teams that want browser-based UI design closer to Figma without proprietary lock-in |
| [Inkscape](https://inkscape.org/) ✅ 🔓 | Free (open source, GPL) ([source](https://inkscape.org/)) | Linux, Windows, macOS | Offline vector UI frames, icons and SVG handoff without an account |
| [Lunacy](https://icons8.com/lunacy) | Free; Personal cloud $4.99/month; Graphics $9.99/month ([source](https://lunacy.docs.icons8.com/subscriptions/)) | Windows, macOS, Linux, Web browser | Desktop UI design with Figma import and optional offline use |
| [Affinity](https://www.affinity.studio/) | Free for individuals (Canva account required) ([source](https://www.affinity.studio/get-affinity)) | Windows, macOS | Designers who want a native pro vector/pixel/layout app at no seat cost |

## Free alternatives to SketchUp

*Category: CAD · Prices checked 2026-10-14 · [FreeInsteadOf.com →](https://freeinsteadof.com/)*

For parametric CAD on Linux, FreeCAD is the strongest free SketchUp alternative we could actually export with; OpenSCAD fits scripted parts; Blender covers mesh modeling; LibreCAD is 2D-only.

| Tool | Price | Platforms | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [FreeCAD](https://www.freecad.org/) ✅ 🔓 | Free (open source) ([source](https://www.freecad.org/downloads.php)) | Linux, Windows, macOS | Parametric solid modeling, STEP/STL export and mechanical-style CAD |
| [OpenSCAD](https://openscad.org/) ✅ 🔓 | Free (open source, GPL) ([source](https://openscad.org/about.html)) | Linux, Windows, macOS | Scripted, parametric parts for 3D printing and machine components |
| [Blender](https://www.blender.org/) ✅ 🔓 | Free (open source, GPL) ([source](https://www.blender.org/about/license/)) | Windows, macOS, Linux | Mesh modeling, visualization and STL export when CAD history is less important |
| [LibreCAD](https://www.librecad.org/) 🔓 | Free (open source, GPLv2) ([source](https://www.librecad.org/)) | Linux, Windows, macOS | 2D drafting and DXF workflows (floor plans, profiles) |
